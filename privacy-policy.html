Privacy Policy

How we handle information you choose to share while seeking guidance, booking a service or ordering puja materials.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This website does not use a contact form, advertising tracker or non-essential cookie by default. Clicking a WhatsApp link takes you to a service operated by WhatsApp/Meta, whose own privacy terms apply.

1. Who we are

“Rekha Astrology”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the online astrology guidance, devotional support and puja-material service available through rekhaastrology.in. The official contact channel listed on this website is our WhatsApp link.

2. Information we may receive

The website itself does not ask you to submit personal information. If you contact us on WhatsApp, book a service or place an order, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name, phone number, profile information and messages available through WhatsApp.
  • Birth date, birth time, birth place and questions relevant to an astrology consultation.
  • Delivery name, address, PIN code and order details for a physical product.
  • Payment status or transaction reference needed to confirm a payment. We do not ask for or store card PINs, OTPs or banking passwords.
  • Feedback or testimonial content that you choose to share.

3. Why we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to answer an enquiry, describe and provide an agreed service, prepare a personalised reading, coordinate a puja or order, handle payment and delivery, provide support, prevent misuse, keep necessary business records, or comply with law.

4. Sensitive and birth-related information

Birth details and personal questions can be sensitive. Share only what is necessary and what you are comfortable providing. You may ask us not to use particular information before the agreed work begins, although this may limit the service we can provide.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum necessary information with service providers involved in communication, payment, packaging, delivery, technology support or legal compliance. WhatsApp/Meta, payment providers and couriers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose described above, including support, accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations. You may request deletion of information we control, subject to records we must retain by law or for legitimate dispute prevention.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical precautions. No internet or messaging method is completely secure. Never send an OTP, card PIN, online-banking password or other authentication secret.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults. A person under 18 should use the service only with the involvement and consent of a parent or lawful guardian.

9. Your choices

You may ask what information we hold, request correction or deletion, withdraw a testimonial permission, or raise a privacy concern by contacting us through the official WhatsApp link. We may need to verify that the request relates to you.

10. Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The date at the top shows the latest published version.

11. Contact

For a privacy request, contact Rekha Astrology on WhatsApp and write “Privacy request” at the beginning of your message.
