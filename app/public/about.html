What you can do

Choose your language, enter your name and date of birth, and talk privately about your questions. Customers communicate with Rekha; there are no customer groups or chats with other customers.

You can share a message or a file you choose. Where available, you can send a voice note, take a photo, or start a voice or video call after granting the required device permission.

The current preview

The app may send sample replies, preset messages, introductions and testimonial collections. The owner can also read conversations and reply personally. A full kundli has not been calculated by this preview.

Advertisements are currently disabled. The ₹49 unlock is a free demonstration; it does not collect a real payment.

Guidance and expectations

Astrology is interpretive guidance. It does not guarantee future events or replace qualified professional advice. Read the disclaimer before relying on a reply.

Your information

The service owner can read the details and messages you send. Share only what is needed. You can change optional choices and delete your conversation. See the privacy policy and deletion instructions.
