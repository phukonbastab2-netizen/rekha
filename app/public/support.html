Install on Android

  1. Open the home page and tap Download the app.
  2. Wait for the download to finish. Tap the download notice, or open Files / My Files → Downloads → RekhaAstrology.apk.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. Choose your language and follow the signup steps.

The app requires Android 8 or newer and an internet connection. Download it only from the official website.

iPhone / iPad: add to Home Screen

ग्राहक ऐप खोलें · Open customer app

Add astrorani.rekhaastrology.in after opening the customer app. Use Safari for these steps. Android APK files do not install on iPhone or iPad.

  1. Open Safari and visit the customer app. If this page is inside another app, open Safari yourself and enter astrorani.rekhaastrology.in.
  2. Tap the upward-arrow Share button. It may be inside Safari's menu. On iPad, use View More if needed.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If it is missing, scroll to Edit Actions and add this option.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App if it appears.
  5. Keep the name Rekha Astrology and tap Add. Open the new Rekha icon on your Home Screen.

Apple's iPhone instructions · Apple's iPad instructions

The phone asks for installation permission

Tap Settings, allow installation from the browser you used, return to the downloaded file and tap Install. Names vary by phone. Keep Play Protect on. After installation, you can turn that browser's installation permission off again.

If your phone reports a security warning, pause and check the file source. Do not disable Play Protect to force installation.

I want to chat without adding an icon

Open browser chat. You can use Safari without adding a Home Screen icon. To add the icon later, follow the iPhone / iPad steps above.

How do I begin a chat?

Choose your language and watch the full introduction video. Enter your own name and adult date of birth, read the privacy notice, and tap Send if you agree. The app prepares the chat for at least five seconds before opening it.

Your chat begins with a shared kundli image and the introduction sequence. Write a message and tap the send button. Customers only chat privately with Rekha. While the scheduled reading messages are arriving, the composer is temporarily paused and your draft is kept.

A message is slow or has not sent

Check your internet connection. A pending or failed message has not yet been confirmed as delivered. If the message shows Retry, tap it once and wait. Avoid repeatedly sending the same message as a new message.

Keep the app open while sending a file. The owner may need time to answer; a successfully sent message does not promise an immediate reply.

Voice, camera or a file does not work

Tap the voice or camera feature you want to use and approve only its required device permission. To share a file, choose it in the phone's file picker. The app does not read your whole gallery, contacts or SMS.

If a permission was blocked earlier, the app or browser may require you to enable it in its settings. Open the chat's ⋮ menu → Feature access for help. Permission names and prompts depend on your phone. Declining an optional permission does not require you to leave text chat.

How do calls and message alerts work?

For voice and video calls, both people must keep their apps open. Microphone and camera permission are requested when starting or answering the relevant call. The app does not record calls.

Android message alerts are optional: use ⋮ menu → Message alerts. They check periodically and may be delayed by Android; they are not guaranteed instant push notifications.

Does the donation button take money?

No. मैं दान करना चाहता/चाहती हूँ · I want to donate sends an interest message inside your chat. It does not open a payment page or charge you. Ads are currently disabled. Do not send card details, OTPs or banking passwords in chat.

How do I change choices or delete my data?

In chat, open ⋮ → Privacy and conversation settings. You can update the optional choices or select Delete my conversation. Read the deletion guide if you cannot open the app.
