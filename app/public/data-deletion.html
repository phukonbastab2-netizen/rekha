Delete Your App Data

You can delete your conversation in the app, or ask support even if you cannot open it.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Request deletion by email

You can contact the operator, Rekha Astrology Pvt Ltd, without installing or signing in to the app. Email chat@rekhaastrology.in with the subject “Data deletion request”.

Request deletion by email

Include the profile name and approximate date you started the chat. If needed to locate the correct chat, privately provide the date of birth entered in the app. Support may ask for the minimum additional information needed to confirm that the conversation belongs to you.

The link opens your email app. Review and send the email yourself; it does not automatically submit a request. Never send passwords, session cookies, access tokens, OTPs, full card details, PINs or banking passwords. You may instead use the in-app or WhatsApp options below.

ऐप के बिना भी डेटा मिटाने के लिए chat@rekhaastrology.in पर ईमेल करें। विषय “Data deletion request” रखें और ईमेल खुद भेजें। पासवर्ड या OTP न भेजें।

अपना डेटा कैसे मिटाएँ?

चैट में ऊपर ⋮ दबाएँ → “Privacy and conversation settings” → “Delete my conversation” → पुष्टि करें। ऐप नहीं खुलता तो नीचे WhatsApp बटन दबाएँ और “Data deletion request” लिखें।

Option 1: delete inside the app

  1. Open your Rekha Astrology conversation.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top.
  3. Choose Privacy and conversation settings.
  4. Tap Delete my conversation.
  5. Read the confirmation, then choose Yes, delete my chat.

This permanently removes that app chat profile, birth details, messages, attached files and associated chat records from the app's active storage. The chat session ends. You cannot restore it using the app.

Option 2: request deletion without the app

You can use this public support link even when the app is uninstalled or the session is lost. Begin your message with “Data deletion request”.

Request deletion on WhatsApp

Include the profile name and approximate date you started the chat. If needed to locate the correct conversation, you can privately provide the date of birth entered in the app. Do not send your full chat history unless specifically needed to resolve the request.

Support may ask for the minimum additional information needed to confirm that the conversation belongs to you. This protects other customers from unauthorized deletion. Opening the link does not automatically send a request; send the message yourself. No fixed processing time is promised.

Never send a password, session cookie, access token, OTP, card number, PIN or banking password.

What deletion does and does not remove

  • Whole conversation: the profile, chat messages, uploaded attachments and linked app records are deleted.
  • A single message: “Delete for everyone” replaces that message with a deletion notice. It is not the same as deleting the full conversation; an uploaded file may remain until the whole chat is deleted or retention cleanup runs.
  • Copies outside the app: files or exports already downloaded, screenshots and messages sent separately through WhatsApp are separate copies. Deleting an app chat cannot erase them automatically.
  • Bookings or payments: deleting chat data does not automatically cancel a separately agreed service or request a refund. Any records that must be retained for such a service are handled separately under applicable requirements.

Automatic retention and your session

Inactive app conversations are scheduled for cleanup after the current retention period, normally 30 days. The app displays the current period; scheduled cleanup may take additional time.

Clearing cookies or uninstalling the app can remove your access to a chat, but does not by itself delete the server's copy. Use one of the deletion options above. The preview has no cross-device account recovery.

For other requests, such as correcting information, see the privacy policy or contact support.
