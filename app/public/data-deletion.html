Option 1: delete inside the app

  1. Connect to the internet and open your Rekha Astrology conversation.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top.
  3. Choose Privacy and conversation settings.
  4. Tap Delete my conversation.
  5. Read the confirmation, then choose Yes, delete my chat.

After the server confirms deletion, the app permanently removes that chat profile, birth details, messages, attached files and associated chat records from active server storage. It also clears this installation's saved device archive, composer draft and outgoing message queue. The chat session ends. You cannot restore it using the app. If the connection fails, deletion is not confirmed; reconnect and try again.

Clear only this device when offline or access has ended

Open the same conversation settings and tap Delete my conversation. When the app is offline or cannot access that server conversation, the confirmation says “Clear saved chat on this device?”. Choose “Clear from this device” to remove this installation's saved history, draft and unsent message queue.

The server copy remains. This option does not submit a server deletion request. Reconnect with valid access to delete the whole conversation, or use the support option below. After clearing, the app shows a cleared-device screen rather than automatically creating a new chat. Reopening the app alone keeps that screen and does not save the chat again. If the server copy still exists and this installation's session is valid, you can choose “Open conversation” to fetch it and begin a fresh device archive. This does not recover a server-deleted chat or restore access after uninstalling. If the device archive could not be cleared, the app reports the failure so you can retry or clear app storage in device settings.

Option 2: request deletion without the app

You can use this public support link even when the app is uninstalled or the session is lost. Begin your message with “Data deletion request”.

Request deletion on WhatsApp

Include the profile name and approximate date you started the chat. If needed to locate the correct conversation, you can privately provide the date of birth entered in the app. Do not send your full chat history unless specifically needed to resolve the request.

Support may ask for the minimum additional information needed to confirm that the conversation belongs to you. This protects other customers from unauthorized deletion. Opening the link does not automatically send a request; send the message yourself. No fixed processing time is promised.

Never send a password, session cookie, access token, OTP, card number, PIN or banking password.

What deletion does and does not remove

  • Whole conversation: the profile, chat messages, uploaded attachments and linked app records are deleted.
  • A single message: “Delete for everyone” replaces that message with a deletion notice. It is not the same as deleting the full conversation; an uploaded file may remain until the whole chat is deleted or retention cleanup runs.
  • Copies outside the app: files or exports already downloaded, screenshots and messages sent separately through WhatsApp are separate copies. Deleting an app chat cannot erase them automatically.
  • Bookings or payments: deleting chat data does not automatically cancel a separately agreed service or request a refund. Any records that must be retained for such a service are handled separately under applicable requirements.

Server retention and your saved device chat

Inactive server conversations are scheduled for cleanup after the current retention period, normally 30 days. The owner can set 7–90 days. The app displays the current period; scheduled cleanup may take additional time.

The app saves previously loaded chat history, unsent messages, composer drafts and files selected for pending messages in private device storage without a timed expiry. Back saves this local conversation before closing the app, and the same installation normally resumes it on reopening. The app checks the server session before sending restored messages. Shared images, videos and other files may still require an online connection and valid private access.

The persistent private session cookie can last up to 365 days and is renewed while you use the connected service; it does not extend server retention or restore a deleted chat. Uninstalling the app or clearing its device/browser storage can remove your saved archive and chat access. There is no cross-device, reinstall or uninstall recovery. The browser or operating system may also clear local data, and saving can fail if storage is full or unavailable.

Clearing local data or uninstalling does not by itself delete the server's copy. Use one of the deletion options above. Copies already downloaded, exported or shared outside the app are separate.

For other requests, such as correcting information, see the privacy policy or contact support.
