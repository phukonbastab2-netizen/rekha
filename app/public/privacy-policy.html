Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Rekha Astrology handles information in its Android app, browser chat and website. It also covers the earlier AstroRani name used by this app.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who handles your information

Rekha Astrology Pvt Ltd operates Rekha Astrology and its private owner panel. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or deletion requests, use the official WhatsApp support link below.

Our support address is chat@rekhaastrology.in. Email delivery is being set up. For now, use WhatsApp for support, privacy and deletion requests.

Contact privacy support on WhatsApp

Opening this link does not send a message automatically. WhatsApp is a separate service, and information you send there is subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

2. Information you provide

  • Chat profile: your name, date of birth, selected language, acceptance of the privacy notice and optional preferences. This preview uses birth details as conversation context; it does not calculate a complete kundli.
  • Conversation: messages, timestamps, delivery and read status, reactions, starred messages, quoted replies and edits. The owner can also keep notes and labels associated with a conversation.
  • Files you choose to share: selected images, videos, documents, voice notes and other supported attachments, including their file names, types and sizes.
  • Optional location: if you choose to share it, your current position is rounded to approximately one decimal degree before storage. It is approximate current location, not your birthplace or continuous location tracking.
  • Support requests: information you voluntarily provide to help locate your chat and resolve an issue.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, banking passwords, full card details, PINs or another person's private information. They are not needed to use the chat.

3. Device and service information

A private, persistent session cookie keeps this installation connected to its chat. The server stores a hash of the session token. The cookie can last up to 365 days and is renewed while you use the connected service. Server access is still required and may end earlier if the conversation is deleted or removed under the retention period below. The preview does not use customer email/password accounts or offer cross-device or reinstall recovery.

The website and app store local preferences, such as language and chat sounds, and cache public app files. They also keep a private device archive in IndexedDB: chat details and messages already loaded, unsent messages, composer drafts and the bytes of files you selected for a queued message or draft. This archive has no automatic time limit and lets the same installation resume its saved conversation, including while offline. It is not an advertising tracker or a substitute for server authorization. Private chat API responses are not placed in the public offline cache.

Hosting services process network requests and operational information, such as IP addresses, browser/device information, request timing and errors, to deliver and protect the service. Session controls, rate limits and security checks help prevent unauthorized access and abuse.

4. Optional permissions

  • Microphone: requested when you choose a voice note or voice call.
  • Camera: requested when you choose a camera or video-call feature.
  • Files and photos: the system picker lets you select individual files to share. The app does not obtain access to your whole gallery.
  • Location: requested only if you select the optional approximate-location choice.
  • Notifications: optional reply alerts may periodically check your own chat in the background. Notifications use a generic message rather than displaying the full private reply.

You can decline optional permissions and still use text chat. A feature that requires a declined permission will not work until you allow it. Android, iOS and browser controls determine when a permission prompt can appear; if previously blocked, you may need to change it in device or browser settings.

The app does not read SMS, contacts, calendars, passwords, banking information, other apps' messages or unrelated device files. Accepting one choice does not grant access to everything on your device.

5. How information is used and shared

We use the information to create and maintain your conversation, deliver replies and shared media, remember your choices, support calls, resolve support requests, enforce retention and deletion choices, and protect the service.

The Rekha Astrology owner can view the profile, messages and attachments in the private owner panel and reply personally or use prepared replies and sequences. Do not share something you do not want the owner to read.

Cloudflare hosts the service. App records are stored in its D1 database and uploaded files in its R2 storage. Hosting providers process the information necessary to operate the service and may process it outside your country. We do not sell chat data.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims. Downloads, exports and screenshots create copies outside the app's storage.

6. Calls

If calling is enabled and you choose to use it, the app uses WebRTC to carry live audio or video. It stores call status and connection information needed to set up the call; connection signals are removed when the call ends. The app currently has no call-recording feature.

Connection services, including Google's default STUN service and any relay service configured for the app, may receive network information such as IP addresses. Network information can also be visible to the other call participant. Declining microphone or camera permission prevents the corresponding media from being shared.

7. Current preview: replies, payments and ads

The current version sends one shared kundli reference image at signup, followed by owner replies and shared content. Previous automatic replies and video flows are paused. It does not send chats to an external AI reply provider. No complete astrological chart is calculated.

Advertising is currently disabled. The current app does not load the AdMob advertising SDK. The ₹49 unlock is a free payment demonstration; no real payment is collected and no card-entry form is provided.

If advertising, a payment provider or an external AI service is introduced, we will update this policy and provide the disclosures and choices needed for that processing before enabling it.

8. Storage and security

Connections use HTTPS. Private chats, files and owner functions have access controls, and session cookies are marked Secure and HttpOnly. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Chats are readable by the service and owner; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Use a trusted device. Clear your session on shared devices and do not share session cookies or access tokens.

The saved device archive can contain private messages and drafts. Protect the device and its browser profile. The app checks the current server session before sending restored messages; a device archive alone cannot give access to a server chat. Previously shared images, videos and other files may need an online connection and valid private access to open again.

9. Retention

Inactive conversations are scheduled for cleanup after the current retention period, normally 30 days. The owner can set a period between 7 and 90 days; the app's privacy settings display the current period. Cleanup runs in scheduled batches and may take additional time.

The device archive has no timed expiry. It can keep previously loaded messages and unsent drafts after server access ends, but cannot recover a server conversation or its files. The browser or operating system can clear local storage, including under storage pressure, and saving can fail if storage is unavailable or full.

Whole-chat deletion requires an online connection. Once the server confirms deletion, the app clears this installation's archive, composer draft and outgoing queue as well as removing the profile, messages, uploaded attachments and associated records from active server storage. Hosting backups and operational logs follow the hosting provider's processes and may not disappear at the same instant. Copies already downloaded, exported or shared through another service are separate.

If you are offline or server access has ended, the same deletion menu offers “Clear from this device”. After your confirmation, this removes only the saved device archive, composer draft and unsent queue. It does not delete the server copy. Reopening the app keeps the cleared-device screen. If the server copy still exists and this installation's session remains valid, choosing “Open conversation” fetches it and starts a fresh device archive. This cannot restore a server-deleted chat. Reconnect with valid access or contact support to request server deletion.

10. Access, correction and deletion

Use the conversation menu's “Privacy and conversation settings” to change optional choices or delete your whole conversation. To ask for access, correct profile information or request deletion without the app, contact privacy support above.

Read the data deletion instructions

Support may ask for the minimum information needed to locate the chat and verify that it belongs to you. Never send a password, OTP, PIN or session token. A single deleted message may leave its uploaded file in storage until whole-chat deletion or retention cleanup.

Closing the app with Back saves the conversation and drafts on this installation; reopening normally resumes them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its device/browser storage can remove the archive and access to the chat. There is no backup code, cross-device recovery or uninstall recovery. These actions do not by themselves delete the server copy. Deletion cannot automatically erase another person's screenshots or exports, or messages sent separately on WhatsApp.

11. Adults only

This app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly accept child chat profiles. If you believe a child has provided information, contact privacy support so it can be investigated and removed.

12. Policy changes

We update the date on this page when the policy changes. Material changes to collection, sharing or permissions will also be explained in the app where appropriate. Use the support link for questions about this policy.
