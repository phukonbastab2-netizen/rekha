The ₹49 preview unlock

The unlock in the current app is a free demonstration. It does not collect money, card details or a bank transfer. Closing the app, stopping a chat or deleting your conversation does not create a refund transaction.

If you believe you were charged in connection with the preview, contact support and explain where the charge appeared. A payment reference or redacted receipt can help. Do not send card numbers, OTPs, PINs or banking passwords.

Separate paid services

A service arranged outside the app, including through WhatsApp, is a separate booking. Before paying, ask for the service scope, final price, planned delivery, cancellation terms and refund conditions in writing.

Whether a booking can be cancelled or refunded depends on what was agreed, work already completed and applicable consumer rights. This page does not impose a blanket “no refund” condition or promise a refund for an unconfirmed service.

How to ask for a cancellation or refund

  1. Open the official support channel.
  2. State “Cancellation request” or “Refund request”.
  3. Include the booking details, relevant reference and reason for the request.

The owner will review the request and the agreed conditions. No fixed refund approval or processing time is promised by the current preview.

Deleting a chat

Data deletion and cancellation are different actions. Deleting your app conversation removes the conversation; it does not automatically cancel a separate booking or issue a payment refund.
