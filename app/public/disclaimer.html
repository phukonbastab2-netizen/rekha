Astrology and outcomes

Astrology is a belief-based, interpretive practice. Statements in the app are not established predictions or guarantees. Do not rely on a reply as proof that an event will happen, that someone has acted in a particular way or that a remedy will produce a specific result.

The preview has not calculated your kundli

The current preview may provide sample replies and preset messages. Entering a name and date of birth does not mean a full kundli has been calculated. A complete chart also requires relevant birth information and a suitable calculation method.

The owner can read and reply to a conversation. Automated samples and owner replies should still be evaluated within the limitations explained here.

Professional and emergency help

The app is not medical, mental-health, legal, financial or emergency care. Do not stop prescribed treatment, spend money, accept a legal obligation or make another significant decision solely because of a chat reply.

If there is immediate danger or an emergency, contact local emergency services or an appropriate professional. The app is not continuously monitored for emergencies.

Testimonials and media

Images, videos and testimonials describe individual experiences or introduce the service. They do not establish typical results, scientific proof or a promise of the same outcome for another person.

Availability and external services

Messages, media and calls depend on devices and internet connections. Features can be unavailable or delayed. External links, including WhatsApp, are subject to the external service's own terms.

Questions or concerns

Contact support if a reply or testimonial concerns you. The terms and privacy policy provide further information.
