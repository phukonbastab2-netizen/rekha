App delivery

The Android app is provided as an APK download from this website. You install it on your own device. Browser chat is available without downloading the APK. See the installation guide for help.

An internet connection is required to load the app's current content and send or receive messages. Download or installation does not mean a paid service has been booked.

Messages and digital content

Replies and shared media appear inside your private conversation. Timing depends on reply mode, owner availability and the connection. The preview does not promise immediate responses, a completed kundli or a fixed consultation schedule.

No physical shipping through this preview

The app preview has no physical-product checkout and does not automatically dispatch puja materials or other goods. No delivery address is required to install the app.

If you separately enquire about a physical product, confirm its availability, contents, final price, delivery charges, serviceable location, expected dispatch and delivery timing, and returns policy before agreeing to buy. Do not assume an advertised or previously discussed item is currently in stock.

Problems or questions

If the APK download or a shared file does not open, use the help guide or contact support. Include the file name and a description of the problem; avoid sending private documents unnecessarily.
