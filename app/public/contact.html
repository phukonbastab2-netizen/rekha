Official support

For app installation, a conversation problem, a privacy question or a deletion request, contact Rekha Astrology through this official WhatsApp channel.

Contact on WhatsApp

The link opens WhatsApp or its website. You decide what to send. Opening the link does not automatically send a message.

Website: rekhaastrology.in
App: Open Rekha Astrology chat

What to include

  • App help: explain what happened, your phone type and the approximate time. A screenshot can help; hide private information first.
  • Privacy question: begin with “Privacy request” and explain your request.
  • Delete app data: begin with “Data deletion request”. Follow the deletion instructions.
  • A separately agreed booking: include the service name and any relevant booking or payment reference.

Do not share passwords, login cookies, access tokens, OTPs, full card details, card PINs or banking passwords.

Support availability

The owner reviews support requests. Response time depends on availability and the information needed to understand the request. Sending a support message does not by itself confirm a booking, payment or refund.

Privacy when using WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a separate service operated by Meta. Its own terms and privacy practices apply when you use it. Share only the information needed for your request.
